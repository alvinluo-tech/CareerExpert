# CMU Computer Science Department — Ph.D. Program Admissions

## Program Overview
The Computer Science Department (CSD) at Carnegie Mellon University offers one of the world's premier doctoral programs in computer science, preparing students to lead the next generation of academic and industrial research in computer vision, robotics, machine learning, and systems.

## Key Evaluation Criteria
1. **Demonstrated Research Potential**: Substantial prior research experience, ability to formulate and solve open scientific problems independently, peer-reviewed publications in top-tier venues (CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, NeurIPS, ICML, SIGGRAPH) highly prioritized;
2. **Academic Excellence & Foundation**: Outstanding mathematical and algorithmic foundations, high GPA in rigorous CS/Math coursework;
3. **Faculty & Lab Alignment**: Strong research alignment with faculty in 3D Vision, Neural Rendering, and Robot Perception (e.g., Prof. Martial Hebert, Prof. Shubham Tulsiani, Prof. Deva Ramanan);
4. **Strong Reference Letters**: Compelling endorsements from active researchers and advisors familiar with the applicant's research contributions.
